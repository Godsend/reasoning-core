## The timeline

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1980 (early)** | Father **Victor Yanenko** arrested for samizdat (circulating unauthorized text). Mother was ~6 months pregnant with Gene at the time. **Sentenced to 13 years.** |
| **1980 (Jul 8)** | Gene born in **Kyiv (Kiev), USSR**. |
| **1986 (Apr)** | **Chernobyl** disaster. Gene + mother moved to **Moscow** for ~1 year with relatives — mother's precaution, "with good reason." |
| **1987** | **Gorbachev freed most political dissidents.** Victor released early — did NOT serve the full 13 years. First in-person meeting between Gene and his father (the gulag was in **Perm**, a long way from Kyiv). |
| **1989 (approx.)** | Family (Gene + Victor + stepmother) left the USSR. Immigration and vetting took time. |
| **1989–90** | Transit: **Vienna** (a couple of months) → **Ladispoli**, a town near Rome (~8 months). |
| **1990** | Arrived in the **US (Brooklyn)**. Gene came with Victor and his stepmother. |
| **~1996** | **Mother finally got to the US.** The stepmother had blocked Gene from sending her an affidavit/relation — she could not come earlier. |
| **~1996** | Gene started working with **Michael Offengeym** at **Microden Systems Inc.** (~age 16), basically dropped out of high school, moved out — and got his mother to the US within about a year of being able to act on it. |

## Corrected vs. earlier versions

- **v1 letter said "imprisoned in 1980 — thirteen years."** The thirteen years was the *sentence*, not the *time served*. He was released in **1987** (Gorbachev amnesty). The contradiction Claude flagged (1980 + 13 = 1993, vs. family in Brooklyn 1990) is resolved: he did not serve the full term.
- The family arrived **1990**, not 1993, because of the early release + the Vienna/Ladispoli transit path.
