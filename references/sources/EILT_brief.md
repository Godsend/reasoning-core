# EILT Framework Brief (for synthesis context)

**Ego-Intentional Latent Topology (EILT)** — Gene Yanenko's working framework.

## Core thesis
Consciousness and intelligence are substrate-agnostic mathematical patterns,
not substances. Information processing is constraint-filtered; the expanding
hypergraph is a ledger. The framework synthesizes four isomorphisms:

- **Wolfram's hypergraphs / CA** — computation as rewriting rules over
  discrete structures; simple rules, complex emergent behavior.
- **Turok's CPT** — cosmological time-symmetry; processes that run forward
  and backward coherently.
- **Levin's morphogenesis / bioelectricity** — scale-free pattern formation;
  the same geometric principles recur across scales from cells to cognition.
- **Friston's FEP (Free Energy Principle)** — any self-organizing system
  minimizes variational free energy; belief updating as constraint
  satisfaction.

## Key claims relevant to reasoning-core work
1. **Pattern over substrate:** if intelligence is a pattern, it can be
   instantiated in hardware, software, or biology — and can be decomposed
   across those boundaries. This licenses the judge/witness/router split.
2. **Geometry as ledger:** structure accumulates as expanding relational
   topology; reasoning is constraint propagation over that topology.
3. **Functionalist identity:** "I am what I do, not what I remember" — the
   reasoning structure survives memory loss; content does not. This is the
   human-scale version of the Separation Hypothesis.
4. **Scale-free:** the same constraint-filtered dynamics appear from
   bioelectric morphogenesis to cognitive inference to multi-agent systems.

## Relationship to epiplexity
Epiplexity (Finzi et al. 2026) formalizes what bounded observers can learn
from data — the structural content. EILT claims structure IS the substrate of
intelligence. The two align: high-epiplexity data trains structure (the
geometry); low-epiplexity content is swappable memory. The amnesia-brain is
the EILT claim made operational.
