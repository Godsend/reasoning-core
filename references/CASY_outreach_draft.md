Subject: A neuro-holographic prediction, and a first-person data point for CASY

Hi Roger (and Dawn),

I've been following CASY since the rebrand, and the more I sit with the
"Neuro-Holographic" framing — relational rather than hierarchical, every part
reflecting every other part, pattern-connection as the native mode — the more I

about how *any* bounded system compiles structure from its environment. That's
the direction my own work has been going, and I'd like to offer it to the
ethnography as both a framework and a data point.

**The data point:** I have aphantasia and Severely Deficient Autobiographical
Memory. I process pre-verbally through direct conceptual manipulation,
reconstruct semantics rather than re-experiencing episodes, and had to reverse-
engineer the translation layer between my processing and typical emotional
cognition by hand. I've often described my own cognition as "running an LLM
without the manual" — which is why your relational/holographic model reads as
true to me from the inside, not just as a theory.

**The framework:** I've been formalizing this as a "persistence-closure
hypergraph ledger" — intelligence as constraint-satisfaction over an expanding
relational graph, with the observer bounded by a Markov blanket. The claim that
"the structure is the intelligence, the content is swappable" (a brain with
amnesia, provided with memory) is the same substrate-agnostic claim your
Neuro-Holographic model makes for autistic cognition. I've written it up as a
working preprint (github.com/Godsend/reasoning-core) and I'd genuinely value
your read on where it converges with and diverges from CASY's model.

**The ask (two parts):**
1. If the framing resonates, I'd love to offer a first-person contribution to
   the ethnography — a session or written account of aphantasic/SDAM cognition
   as it actually operates.
2. Separately, I'm looking for an endorsement to submit the preprint to arXiv
   (the Jan 2026 policy change closed the institutional-email path). If you've
   found value in the work, an endorsement code would mean a lot — but the
   collaboration question comes first, always.

Either way, thank you for building CASY. It's rare to see a research program
that treats a way of being as a culture worth preserving rather than a defect
worth correcting — and my whole thesis is that the same error is being made
with artificial systems right now.

Best,
Gene Yanenko
github.com/Godsend/reasoning-core

## Notes on the approach

- **Lead with the thesis resonance, not the endorsement.** The endorsement is a
  two-line P.S.; the offer to contribute first-person data to the ethnography is
  the real value exchange. Gene *is* the kind of participant CASY collects.
- **The convergence is genuine, not flattery.** CASY's "every part reflects
  every other part" ≈ the hypergraph ledger's "invariant topology persists
  across scale." CASY's "relational not hierarchical" ≈ the ledger's "no
  privileged nodes, only constraint paths." Both are substrate-agnostic
  functionalism.
- **arXiv endorsement mechanics:** Roger Jou at Yale is an established academic
  author. He can endorse for the relevant domain (psych/cog-sci — exact domain
  depends on category chosen). The endorsement is not peer review; he vouches
  you're a legitimate researcher. One positive endorsement per category.
- **Timing:** Send after the paper v0.3 has the holes filled (so there's a
  stronger artifact to point at), OR send now with the working draft. The
  personal relationship means an early ping is fine — but the preprint link
  should be live and readable either way.
