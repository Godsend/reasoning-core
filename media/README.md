## The generation lesson
NotebookLM infographics have a systematic text-rendering bug (garbled glyphs
in ~50% of renders: "measurable"→"ieaniacle", "length"→"Leagth",
"questionnaire"→"Oxestionnone", "decay"→"Desay"). **Explicit instructions
listing the exact words and their correct spellings reliably produces a clean
render on the next pass.** Iterate: generate → vision-verify → regenerate with
the specific corrupted words called out. Do not publish a render without a
vision text check.
